01 A little about me

Making network intelligence more robust.

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Mohan Gurusamy and Dr. Dinil Mon Divakaran. I expect to graduate in December 2026.

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and network security. I’m interested in how traffic can be represented and analyzed in ways that remain useful when data is limited, unfamiliar, or adversarial. Away from research, I enjoy books, films, badminton, and time outdoors.
